Appam

Taste

Appam is a soft, fluffy Kerala pancake with a slightly crispy edge and soft center. It has a mild sweetness and is best served with vegetable stew, chicken curry, or egg curry.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups raw rice
  • 1/2 cup grated coconut
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp yeast
  • Salt (as needed)
  • Water

Recipe

1. Soak the rice in water for 4–5 hours.

2. Grind the soaked rice with grated coconut and water to make a smooth batter.

3. Add sugar and yeast to the batter and mix well.

4. Keep the batter covered and allow it to ferment for 6–8 hours (or overnight).

5. Add salt and mix gently before cooking.

6. Heat an appam pan (appachatti) and pour a ladle of batter.

7. Swirl the pan to spread the batter (thin edges, thick center).

8. Cover and cook for 2–3 minutes until the edges turn crispy.

9. Serve hot with stew or curry.
