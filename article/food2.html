Puttu

Taste

Puttu is a soft and steamed rice cake layered with coconut. It has a mild, slightly sweet taste and is commonly served with kadala curry, banana, or sugar.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice flour
  • 1 cup grated coconut
  • Water (as needed)
  • Salt (to taste)

Recipe

1. Take rice flour in a bowl and add salt.

2. Sprinkle water little by little and mix until the flour becomes moist (not dough).

3. The texture should be crumbly when pressed in hand.

4. Take a puttu maker and add a layer of grated coconut.

5. Add a layer of rice flour, then coconut again (repeat layers).

6. Steam for 5–7 minutes until cooked.

7. Remove from the mould carefully.

8. Serve hot with kadala curry, banana, or sugar.
