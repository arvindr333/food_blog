Idli

Taste

Idli is a soft, fluffy, and steamed South Indian rice cake made from fermented batter. It has a mild taste and is commonly served with coconut chutney and sambar.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice
  • 1/2 cup urad dal
  • 1/2 tsp fenugreek seeds (optional)
  • Salt (to taste)
  • Water (as needed)

Recipe

1. Soak rice, urad dal, and fenugreek seeds separately for 4–6 hours.

2. Grind them into a smooth batter and mix together.

3. Add salt and allow the batter to ferment overnight.

4. Grease the idli plates with a little oil.

5. Pour the batter into the idli moulds.

6. Steam for 10–12 minutes until cooked.

7. Remove and allow it to cool slightly.

8. Serve hot with chutney and sambar.
