Dosa

Taste

Dosa is a thin, crispy South Indian crepe made from fermented rice and urad dal batter. It has a light, slightly tangy taste and is commonly served with coconut chutney and sambar.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice
  • 1/2 cup urad dal
  • 1/2 tsp fenugreek seeds (optional)
  • Salt (to taste)
  • Water (as needed)
  • Oil (for cooking)

Recipe

1. Soak rice, urad dal, and fenugreek seeds separately for 4–6 hours.

2. Grind them into a smooth batter and mix together.

3. Add salt and allow it to ferment overnight.

4. Heat a dosa tawa and pour a ladle of batter.

5. Spread it thin in a circular motion.

6. Drizzle oil around the edges.

7. Cook until the dosa becomes crispy and golden brown.

8. Fold and serve hot with chutney and sambar.
