Porotta

Taste

Porotta is a soft, flaky, and layered flatbread popular in Kerala. It has a slightly crispy outside and soft inside, and is best served with beef curry, chicken curry, or vegetable curry.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups maida (all-purpose flour)
  • Water (as needed)
  • Salt (to taste)
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional)
  • 2 tbsp oil

Recipe

1. Take maida in a bowl and add salt, sugar, and oil.

2. Add water slowly and knead into a soft, smooth dough.

3. Cover and rest the dough for at least 1–2 hours.

4. Divide the dough into small balls.

5. Roll each ball into a thin sheet and stretch it as much as possible.

6. Fold the sheet into layers (like a spiral).

7. Roll it again gently into a round shape.

8. Heat a tawa and cook both sides until golden brown.

9. Press and crush lightly to make layers soft and flaky.

10. Serve hot with curry.
