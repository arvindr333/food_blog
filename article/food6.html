Masala Dosa

Taste

Masala dosa is a crispy, golden brown South Indian crepe made from fermented rice and urad dal batter. It is filled with a flavorful potato masala and served with chutney and sambar.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice
  • 1/2 cup urad dal
  • Salt (to taste)
  • Water (as needed)
  • Oil (for cooking)
  • For Masala Filling:
  • 3–4 boiled potatoes
  • 1 onion (sliced)
  • 2 green chilies
  • 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
  • Curry leaves
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt

Recipe

1. Soak rice and urad dal separately for 4–6 hours.

2. Grind them into a smooth batter and mix together.

3. Add salt and allow it to ferment overnight.

4. Heat a dosa tawa and pour a ladle of batter, spreading it thin in circular motion.

5. Drizzle oil and cook until crispy and golden.

6. For masala: heat oil, add mustard seeds, curry leaves, onion, and green chilies.

7. Add turmeric, salt, and mashed potatoes. Mix well.

8. Place the masala inside the dosa and fold it.

9. Serve hot with coconut chutney and sambar.
