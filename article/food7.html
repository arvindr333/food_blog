Idiyappam

Taste

Idiyappam is a soft and light steamed rice noodle dish. It has a mild taste and is commonly served with coconut milk, sugar, vegetable curry, or egg curry.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice flour
  • Hot water (as needed)
  • Salt (to taste)
  • 1 tsp oil (optional)

Recipe

1. Take rice flour in a bowl and add salt.

2. Pour hot water slowly and mix well to form a soft dough.

3. Knead the dough until smooth.

4. Grease the idiyappam press with a little oil.

5. Fill the dough into the press.

6. Press the dough into thin noodles onto an idli plate or steamer tray.

7. Steam for 8–10 minutes until cooked.

8. Remove and serve hot with coconut milk, sugar, or curry.
