Chappathi

Taste

Chappathi is a soft, thin flatbread made from wheat flour. It has a mild taste and is commonly eaten with curry, vegetables, or chicken dishes.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups wheat flour (atta)
  • Water (as needed)
  • Salt (optional)
  • 1 tsp oil (optional)

Recipe

1. Take wheat flour in a bowl and add salt (optional).

2. Add water slowly and knead into a soft dough.

3. Cover and rest the dough for 15–20 minutes.

4. Divide the dough into small balls.

5. Roll each ball into a thin circle using a rolling pin.

6. Heat a pan (tawa) and place the rolled chapati on it.

7. Cook both sides until brown spots appear and it puffs up.

8. Apply a little oil or ghee if needed.

9. Serve hot with curry or vegetables.
